What We Build

Everything a startup needs to win government business, built in this order and handed over running. Engage us for the full build or the one piece you are missing.

Founders want the division overnight. We build it in six phases, in this order, and each one has to hold before the next goes on.

01

Administrative Foundation

The back office that lets a small team run a federal business.

  • AI-powered operations dashboard: pipeline, contracts, cash, and compliance deadlines on one screen
  • Agents on the daily work: proposals drafted, invoices out, registrations kept current
  • Lean staffing model: the few people you hire, and what the agents carry instead
  • Finance and compliance rails: DCAA-ready accounting, timekeeping, and policies from day one
Phase One

02

Go-to-Market

Who buys, how they buy, and what has to be true first.

  • Target agencies: who actually buys what you built
  • Capture strategy: how a deal is won before it is posted
  • Registrations and pricing: SAM.gov, CAGE, NAICS, and rates that pass review
  • Compliance: CMMC and FedRAMP, the gates startups hit late
Phase Two

03

Product Strategy

What you built, made buyable by a government customer.

  • Requirements mapping: your roadmap against what agencies are required to buy
  • Authorization path: FedRAMP, ATO, IL levels; which one, and what it costs you
  • Pilot to program: designing the first pilot so it can become a program of record
  • Differentiation: the claims that survive a source-selection board
Phase Three

04

Partnerships

Most federal revenue reaches a startup through somebody else’s contract.

  • Primes and integrators: who already holds the vehicles you need
  • Teaming agreements: signed, with scope and exclusivity defined up front
  • Vehicles and set-asides: GSA, GWACs, OTAs, SBIR; the fastest route in
  • Channel economics: margin, pass-through, and who owns the customer
Phase Four

05

Vetted Talent

A division is people. We put the right ones in seat.

  • Capture and BD leads: operators who have run the play before
  • Cleared personnel: where the work requires it, sourced and processed
  • Contracts and compliance: the back office that keeps you eligible
  • Interim leadership: we run the seat until your hire is ready
Phase Five

06

Government Affairs Strategy

The campaign that turns one contract into a market.

  • Agency engagement: program offices, contracting shops, and the people who write requirements
  • Budget and policy: getting your capability into the line items that fund it
  • Coalitions and associations: allies who carry the argument when you cannot
  • International: allied governments, FMS, and export controls when you scale abroad

Leadership

Brad Belzak

Founder

Acuity Global Partners is led by Brad Belzak, a senior executive and advisor with 24 years across government, defense, technology, consulting, finance, and global risk — supported by vetted specialists in law, tax, security, investigations, and local-market advisory.

His career has moved between public service and the private sector for two decades. He began at the Department of Homeland Security in its earliest days, holding progressive roles across intelligence, disaster response, and policy — FEMA during Hurricane Katrina, the FBI Terrorism Screening Center, and aviation security at TSA — and performed security counsel and investigations for a U.S. Senator on the U.S. Senate Homeland Security Committee in Congress. He then advised governments and multinationals through crisis and growth at Deloitte, Crumpton Group, and EY, from the 2011 Thai floods and post-conflict Libya to risk and investigations across Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

He returned to government to direct homeland defense integration at the Pentagon, where he helped lead the U.S. response to the Chinese spy balloon incident, then served as second-in-command of the Export-Import Bank and helped build the public sector practice at Strider Technologies. He currently serves as a Commissioner on the Washington Metrorail Safety Commission, holds degrees from Northeastern and Elon, and is a Certified Fraud Examiner.
