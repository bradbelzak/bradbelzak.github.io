Leadership

Brad Belzak

CEO & Founder

Brad Belzak founded Acuity Global Partners after 24 years in senior national security roles in Washington and managing risk on the ground worldwide.

In Washington

  • Second-in-command of the Export-Import Bank of the United States, with a $135 billion financing portfolio and deep, trusted relationships across the globe.
  • Directed homeland defense policy at the Pentagon, including the U.S. policy response to the Chinese spy balloon and the Department's counter-drone portfolio.
  • Built the civilian government business at Strider Technologies, including a 16-partner sales network of consulting firms and integrators.
  • Chaired the D.C. Government's Homeland Security Commission, co-authoring its cybersecurity and mass care reports.
  • Directed legislative affairs for the DHS Office of Intelligence and Analysis, working with Congress through national security crises.
  • Co-wrote the Aviation Security Innovation and Reform Act as professional staff on the Senate Homeland Security Committee.
  • Began his career at DHS as a counterterrorism analyst detailed to the FBI Terrorism Screening Center.

In the Field

  • Led risk, compliance, and investigative engagements as a Big Four consultant across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, including business development with the United Nations.
    • Helped build a global crisis management practice, from Thai flood continuity planning in 2011 to the Hurricane Sandy crisis office.
    • Worked in post-conflict settings, including Libya after the fall of Qaddafi, and wrote security policies for the U.S. Embassy in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
    • Served as chief of staff to EY's global forensics leader.
  • Performed travel security and risk management projects in the United States, Asia, and Mexico at Crumpton Group.
  • Conducted U.S. Senate oversight investigations in the Middle East, Europe, and North Africa, including security assessments at 16 international airports.
  • Served as a FEMA first responder after Hurricane Katrina, then as deputy regional director for federal Gulf Coast rebuilding.
  • Assessed country risk worldwide as an analyst in the DHS Visa Waiver Program office.

He is a Nonresident Senior Fellow at the Atlantic Council, a member of the International Institute for Strategic Studies, a Commissioner on the Washington Metrorail Safety Commission, and a Certified Fraud Examiner, and holds an M.S. in Security and Resiliency from Northeastern University and a B.A. in Political Science from Elon University.
